tiny-paper display: sketch

Draw with the mouse, a pen or a finger. Each stroke goes to the panel as a partial update of the area that changed; the drawing here is what the panel is brought to.

This browser cannot reach a USB device (no Web Serial). Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. See the page work without a device.

Plug the display in with a USB data cable, press Connect and pick its port ("USB JTAG/serial debug unit").

This page needs the display firmware on the device: install it on the Flash page, then come back.

No device at hand: the page with a pretend TRMNL X, TRMNL OG or PicPak. What the device answers to a stroke is explained in the guide.
